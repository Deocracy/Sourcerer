    State the general rules once, plainly:

    - The canonical printed form is the opaque `scheme:path`. The authority form
      is never emitted, because its authority component is lower-cased on
      serialization and Theia's widget ids are not uniformly lowercase.
    - Parsing is lenient: the four typeable spellings all reduce to one name.
      Emission is canonical: one form comes back out. This is why an address
      copied out of the app and typed back in by hand behaves identically.
    - The path is identity and identity only — no query, no fragment, no encoded
      payload. Anything a human copies must survive copy-paste exactly, and the
      simplest way to guarantee that is to have nothing in the address that
      needs encoding.
    - Non-user-chosen discriminators stay in widget options and never appear in
      an address.

    the coverage boundary explicitly: every widget that owns a shell tab has an

    sub-views inside a container are collapsible sections rather than tabs and
    are deliberately out of scope for v4.0, addressable later at no design cost.
