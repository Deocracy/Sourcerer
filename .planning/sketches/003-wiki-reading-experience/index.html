RMS Titanic

curated
318 canonical claims · head v14 · composed on demand · every claim traceable

RMS Titanic was a British ocean liner operated by the White Star Line. She departed Southampton on her maiden voyage on 10 April 1912, bound for New York with 2,435 passengers and 892 crew aboard.

The ship carried 20 lifeboats with a total capacity of 1,178 people — fewer than half of those aboard. Her exact departure time is disputed between noon and 12:15 pm across ingested sources.

The wreck lies at a depth of 3,784 metres and was located in 1985 by a Franco-American expedition.
